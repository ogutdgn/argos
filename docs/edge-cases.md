## Visitor leaves for a short time

The visitor steps out (for example to the restroom) for around 10 minutes and comes back. By the rule in [flows.md](flows.md) Flow C (visitor not heard for 5 minutes), the conversation would end early. How to tell a short absence from the real end of a visit is not decided yet.
